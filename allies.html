04 · Allies & Funders

With us. Beside us. Supporting all our children.

Whether you’re an individual standing beside us in this work, or an institution choosing to extend real support — the same principle holds: amplify Indigenous voices, never replace them. Allies make room. That’s the whole job, and it’s enough.

UNIPC holds a place for allies, advocates, and individual supporters — kindred in this work, not evaluators of it.

Allies extend our reach — translation, research, logistics, introductions, and the patient work of correcting the record wherever Indigenous institutions are misdescribed.

For those moved to support us materially, see Support the Recognition — the same in-kind principle applies here as everywhere else in UNIPC’s finances: nothing reaches us that could reach for influence in return.

Allies do not vote, do not speak for nations, and do not set direction — yet are always considered vital to our advancement.

Internships & volunteering.

UNIPC’s preparatory work — including everything on this website — is already involving great hands-on experience, carried out entirely by volunteers: at least half a dozen active interns today, with many more having contributed over the years, including some of our co-founders.

We welcome university students — particularly in political science, international relations, communications, media and journalism, law and human rights, linguistics and education, and many other related fields — to intern with us now: real, practical experience supporting Indigenous Peoples and their advancement, in how an international organization is actually built from its earliest days, before it has funding or staff to do that work for you elsewhere.

Like every role at UNIPC today, this is unpaid and consistent with our Preparatory Phase — but the work is real, and it’s happening now. Who knows where the future finds you — perhaps a full-time position abroad, or a seat in the civil service.
