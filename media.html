Media room & brand use

Describe us accurately. It is the whole ask.

Everything a journalist, partner, or member needs to represent UNIPC correctly — the mark, the palette, the type, and the one rule that governs how we are named. Use it freely, within the register below.

How to refer to us.

Say this
  • ✓UNIPC — United Nations Indigenous Peoples Council, an organization of Indigenous nations and peoples.
  • ✓A council of Indigenous Nations and Peoples, founded in 2020.
  • ✓Presently a preparatory initiative — designing the instruments of a future Indigenous diplomacy.
Not this
  • ×Not an “intergovernmental organization” — that status follows from state action (treaty ratification), not self-description.
  • ×Not part of, controlled by, or directed by the United Nations Organization — UNIPC is entirely independent.
  • ×Not yet an operating organization — it holds no funds, staff, or operations.

The tense signal

Every claim is written in one of three tenses and marked so readers can tell fact from ambition.

ISWhat we are — present, verifiable.
ACHIEVEDWhat we achieved — past, on the record.
SEEKSWhat we seek — ambition, never implied as fact.
