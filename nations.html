01 · Nations & Peoples

You are not joining someone else’s table.

UNIPC was founded by more than sixty individual co-founders, drawn from different Indigenous Nations across many regions where you call home, convened as one assembly — a founding of people, not yet of Nations bound by formal agreement, exactly as any assembly must begin.

The ones who built it faced what you face: papers that don’t name your nation, or recognize your indigeneity, banks that don’t know your address, borders drawn through your homeland, and rooms where decisions about you are made without you.

Within UNIPC, we are here to bring your voice forward — the voice of our united Nations as decision makers — to the forefront of other international decision-makers.

What this is, plainly.

Today

UNIPC is where nations gather to deliberate. We hold assemblies. We are writing a treaty of friendship between Indigenous nations. We are designing a registry to hold Indigenous treaties safely — yours, your grandparents’, and the ones your nation will make next — for activation once UNIPC is operating.

Tomorrow

Identity and travel documents that carry your nation’s name — issued only after the states involved agree to honor them. Financial services under real regulation. These are programs in design. We will tell you they exist only when they truly do. That is a promise.

Membership costs you nothing that is yours.

  • Your sovereignty stays yours. Joining takes no position on your nation’s status and waives no claim.
  • Your voice stays yours. Where your nation and UNIPC are both in a room, your nation speaks first.
  • Your treaties stay yours. The registry holds them; it never owns them.
  • Your say is real. Indigenous nations, tribal governments, traditional authorities, and our Active Co-Founding Members in Good Standing hold the vote.

And if your nation lives inside a state that denies you exist: we have a protected path. Quiet accession. Names shielded. We were built by people who know why that matters.
