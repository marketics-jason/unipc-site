08 · The path to recognition

We publish our road because we intend to be held to it.

Functions first, instruments second, status as the record’s conclusion. No stage claims the next.

  1. 1

    Constitute

    Largely complete

    The charter among nations, the council, the assembly — designed in full; awaiting activation.

  2. 2

    Build track record

    Treaty registry design finalized. Standards published. Convenings held, in co-founders’ own capacity. Recognition follows entities that already work.

  3. 3

    Treaty

    The Multilateral Treaty of Friendship, Cooperation and Recognition opened for signature — nations first, then states as counterparts.

  4. 4

    Seat

    A headquarters agreement with a host state: legal personality, protected archives, the address diplomacy requires.

  5. 5

    Acceptance, instrument by instrument

    Member documentation presented to each recognizing ministry on its own merits. Financial services announced only under license. Every acceptance is recorded. The record is the argument.

No stage claims the next. When this page says a stage is complete, an instrument exists to prove it — and we can back it.
