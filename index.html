In October 2020, more than sixty co-founders from Indigenous Nations and Peoples across diverse countries established UNIPC. The council now works to create a permanent place where Indigenous nations can welcome and meet the world as equals.

The thirty-second institution

What we are, what we achieved, and what we seek — each in its own tense, never blended.

What we are

A council of Indigenous Nations and Peoples, founded in 2020, governed by our growing members, organized across all inhabited continents. Presently a preparatory initiative — not to be incorporated but recognized as a bona fide international organization; not yet an operating organization.

At that founding General Assembly, our co-founding Members also voted to designate who will carry each key role within UNIPC — each holding that title as “[Role]-designate” until treaty ratification formally seats the office. This mirrors established diplomatic and organizational practice, where an appointment can be affirmed well before the institution itself achieves full recognition.

What we achieved

We convened. Our General Assembly met virtually, resolved, and appointed senior roles-designate, since 2020.

We approved a draft, with final revisions, of the Treaty of Tiohtiá:ke — a Multilateral Treaty of Friendship, Cooperation and Recognition, open for signature by States and International Organizations willing to stand publicly with Indigenous Peoples.

We built the UNIPC Constitutional Architecture and Framework for Recognition: standards for consent and documentation, and protocols for how nations and states meet with respect.

What we seek

A seat at the table — what diplomats would call standing — secured the way institutions built to last actually secure it: through sequence, precedent, and accumulated recognition, not declaration.

  1. 01
    A headquarters agreement with a strategically accepted host state.
  2. 02
    Treaty ratification by states across continents.
  3. 03
    Increased Nation membership by way of a Nation Treaty of Friendship and Unity.
  4. 04
    Regulated financial services, obtained through proper licensing — and travel documents that borders actually honor.

We publish the road because not only do we intend to be held to it, but we will also walk the road before you have to.
