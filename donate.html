05a · How to donate

A gift, if you’d like to give one.

UNIPC itself holds no funds and accepts no donations directly — consistent with our Preparatory Phase. Instead, a small number of independently registered, mission-aligned non-profit organizations support UNIPC’s preparatory work by receiving funds and directing them, at their own discretion, toward that work.

No payment is collected on this website, and none will be until the proper mechanism is formally in place. There is no form here to fill in and no account to send money to — by design. Read our current status →

On tax receipts — read this before you give.

Tax-receipt eligibility depends on your own jurisdiction’s rules, not just the receiving organization’s status — most countries don’t recognize a charitable receipt issued under a different jurisdiction’s tax code.

Where a receipt isn’t available, giving this way is genuinely humanitarian, freely offered without expectation of anything in return — and that, more than any tax benefit, is exactly the spirit we hope draws people to it.

Confirm receipt eligibility for your own situation directly with the specific organization before donating if that matters to your decision.
