02 · States & Ministries

Precision is our protocol.

UNIPC is a council of Indigenous nations and peoples, constituted in 2020, presently designing the instruments of a future Indigenous diplomacy. We are candid about what we are not yet — and we submit that this candor is the strongest credential an emerging institution can present.

The category, correctly stated

No overclaim appears on this page. That is the point.

What we are not

We do not claim intergovernmental status. Under the instruments that define the term, that status follows from state action — treaty ratification — not self-declaration.

The precedented road

Our road is the functional one, and it is precedented: entities from the ICRC to the Sovereign Military Order of Malta acquired international standing through exercised mandates, host-state arrangements, and accumulated recognition — not through a founding claim. Functions first, instruments second, status as the record’s conclusion.

What engagement offers a ministry.

A single, organized counterpart for Indigenous diplomacy: a council spanning all inhabited continents, a treaty process in motion, a treaty registry function in design, and protocols built so that states and nations meet without prejudice to any position on status or territory.

Nothing in our instruments may be construed against the territorial integrity or political unity of states.

The quiet dividend

Recognition carries its own quiet dividend: the States that welcome Indigenous Nations first are naturally the ones our members — and their own peoples — gravitate toward, in the everyday choices millions of individuals make about where to trade, to study, to travel, and to feel genuinely accepted.

UNIPC does not need to promise this. It only needs to open the door.

In order — no omnibus asks, no surprises
  1. 01
    Participation in convenings.
  2. 02
    Treaty conversation.
  3. 03
    Trade and tourism benefits.
  4. 04
    In time, acceptance of member documentation — ministry by ministry, each instrument on its own review.
