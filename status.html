Preparatory phase

Where we actually stand today.

UNIPC is presently a preparatory initiative — not to be incorporated, but recognized as a bona fide international organization. It is not yet an operating organization. This page states plainly what that means, so nothing on this website can be mistaken for more than it is.

No funds

UNIPC holds no funds or bank accounts and accepts no monetary contributions of any kind.

No staff

Every role today is unpaid. The work is carried out entirely by volunteers and co-founders.

No operations

The offices themselves remain dormant until treaty ratification formally seats them.

No physical office

Correspondence is handled by co-founders and representatives in their own capacity.

Why we designed it this way

Holding no funds is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight — made specifically so no one can claim to represent UNIPC while handling money on its behalf.

An institution with no operating seats has nothing for anyone to falsely claim to hold.

Roles-designate

At the founding General Assembly, our co-founding Members voted to designate who will carry each key role within UNIPC — each holding that title as “[Role]-designate” until treaty ratification formally seats the office. This mirrors established diplomatic and organizational practice, where an appointment can be affirmed well before the institution itself achieves full recognition.

Accounting discipline, ahead of requirement

UNIPC has voluntarily committed to International Public Sector Accounting Standards from its earliest operating years — the same accounting discipline used by major intergovernmental organizations — well before any external requirement compels it. Once UNIPC holds funds, that same discipline governs every contribution: unearmarked or declined, and no single funder positioned to define our direction.
